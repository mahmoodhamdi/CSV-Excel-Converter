# Support Plans

Last updated: 2026-05-13

Support is included for the duration listed in each pricing tier. Extended support is sold separately.

## Tiers

| Tier | Response SLA | Channels | Hours | Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Community** | Best effort | GitHub issues, public Discord | — | Free |
| **Standard** | 48h business hours | Email | Mon–Thu 9–17 EET | $200/month |
| **Priority** | 8h business hours | Email + private Slack channel | Mon–Thu 9–17 EET | $600/month |
| **Enterprise** | 1h critical / 4h non-critical | Email + Slack + phone | 24/7/365 | $2,000/month |

## Severity definitions

| Severity | Definition | Example | Initial response |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1 Critical** | Production system down or data loss | API returning 500 on every request | Enterprise: 1h, Priority: 2h |
| **S2 High** | Major feature broken, no workaround | Excel export corrupts large files | Enterprise: 4h, Priority: 8h |
| **S3 Medium** | Feature broken with workaround | Specific encoding fails to auto-detect | Enterprise: 1 business day, Priority: 2 business days |
| **S4 Low** | Cosmetic, documentation, feature request | Typo in error message | Best effort |

## What's covered

- ✅ Bug fixes for current and previous major version
- ✅ Configuration help for deployments matching our documented setups
- ✅ Custom field mapping guidance (for vertical tiers)
- ✅ Upgrade assistance during major version transitions
- ✅ Security advisory notifications

## What's not covered

- ❌ Custom feature development (sold as separate engagement)
- ❌ Performance tuning of customer infrastructure (e.g., your Postgres instance)
- ❌ Training beyond the 1-hour onboarding for Pro tier
- ❌ Integration with third-party systems we don't list as supported

## Escalation

If a Priority/Enterprise customer's SLA is missed, escalate to:

1. Initial responder (email)
2. Engineering on-call (Slack)
3. Founder (phone — Enterprise only)

We credit one full month's support fee per SLA miss, no questions.
